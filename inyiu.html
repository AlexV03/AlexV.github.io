About

In my second year at BUAS, together with a team we created It's not you it's us(INYIU). The designers had 8 weeks to comeup with a game prototype, then we had 8 weeks with all the disaplines to make the full game. The game is a 3D point and click adventure, where you play as different characters each with there own ability. You explore the town and using your ability you take care of the town.

Creation I Made

With this project I was the lead programmer, while managing the other programmers and deciding on decissions with the other leads and the producer, when i had the time I worked on some smaller features. I made the camera follow functionality, with a lot of custimizibility for the designers to use, Camera zooming in and out when interacting with other characters, and fixing bugs.
