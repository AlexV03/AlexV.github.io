About

In my second year I made a raytraced audio feature in c++. The goal was to make audio more realistic, with features that happen in the real world. The raytraced audio supports multiple audio features including, Stereo panning, Occlusion, Echo and Transmission. Rays are shot from the audiosources and when they bounch around they, reflect from the environment and there energy changes based on the material it hit. If the ray hits the player the audio gets processed.
